  1. Create a project. Open New project, name it "Data Cleaner" and press Create. Check that the project picker at the top of the page shows it before you go on.
  2. Turn on Gmail. Open the Gmail API and press Enable.
  3. Describe your app. Open Google Auth Platform and press Get started. App name: Data Cleaner. Support email and contact email: your own address. Audience: External. Agree to the policy and press Create. Leave the logo empty: a logo makes Google want to review the app.
  4. Let yourself in. Open Audience. Under Test users, press Add users, enter the address you'll connect and press Save. Skipping this is what causes "Access blocked: Data Cleaner has not completed the Google verification process".
  5. Allow mail access. Open Data Access and press Add or remove scopes. In Manually add scopes at the bottom, paste gmail.send and gmail.readonly , then press Add to table, Update and Save.
  6. Create the sign-in client. Open Create client. Application type: Web application. Under Authorized redirect URIs, press Add URI and paste {{ redirect_uri }} . Press Create.
  7. Copy the client ID and secret from the window that opens, paste them below and save. Google shows the secret only once. If you lose it, add a new one on the client's page.
  8. Connect. Google will say it "hasn't verified this app". That's expected: it's your own private app, not a public one. Press Continue.

While the app is in Testing, Google asks you to reconnect every 7 days; Data Cleaner shows "needs reconnecting" in Settings when it's time. Pressing Publish app on Audience ends that, but Google first wants a home page and privacy policy link on the Branding page.
