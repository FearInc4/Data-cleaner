Import a state data broker registry

States that require data brokers to register publish their contact details. Importing a registry adds hundreds of brokers with their privacy emails and deletion links, which Data Cleaner can then email automatically.

Columns are matched by name (broker name, website, email, deletion/opt-out link), so files from different years work. Curated catalog entries are never overwritten; imports only fill gaps.

Where to download

  • California: cppa.ca.gov/data_broker_registry → "Download CSV". The largest registry, with deletion links for every broker.
  • Vermont: Secretary of State business search, filtered to data brokers.
  • Texas: Secretary of State data broker registry.
  • Oregon: Department of Financial Regulation data broker registry.
  • All four, merged: Privacy Rights Clearinghouse database.

Registries are refreshed every year (registration closes Jan 31), so re-import each February.
